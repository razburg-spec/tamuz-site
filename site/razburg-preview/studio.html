Tamuz Studios · Tel Aviv

The Studio

I work out of Tamuz Studios, the recording and mixing studio I founded in Tel Aviv in 2006. It is acoustically treated throughout and built for tracking bands, vocals, voice-over and podcasts, and for mixing and mastering.

Studio A

A control room and a live recording room, for full band tracking, vocals and acoustic instruments.

Studio B

A second control room linked to Studio A, so we can record live across all spaces at once.

Remote work

Mixing and mastering are also done remotely: send stems, get mixes back with notes and revisions online.
