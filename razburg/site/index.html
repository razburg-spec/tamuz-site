What I do

  • Mixing & Mastering

    Mixes that translate on every system, and masters ready for streaming, vinyl and broadcast. Attended or remote sessions.

  • Music Production

    From demo to finished record: arrangement, recording, editing and production for artists and bands, in any genre.

  • Sound Design & Post

    Sound design, editing and post-production for film, TV, games, commercials and podcasts.

  • Original Music

    Scores and library music for film, media and brands. Twenty albums of my music are licensed worldwide through Artlist.
