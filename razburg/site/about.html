About

Raz Burg

I'm a sound designer, composer, music producer and mixing & mastering engineer based in Tel Aviv.

I started out as a kid on piano and synthesizers. In 1998 I built my first home studio to record my own music and the musicians around me, and spent the following years recording in studios and on stage.

In 2006 I founded Tamuz Studios in Tel Aviv. It has since become a home for many artists, bands and producers, and today it also hosts podcasts, voice-over and audiobook work. Over the years I have recorded, produced, mixed and mastered more than a hundred releases across many genres.

Alongside records, I write and design sound for film, commercials and media, and my own instrumental albums are licensed worldwide through Artlist. I also develop audio plugins under the name Raz Burg AudioCrafts.

My aim is simple: give every artist everything their music needs to sound its best, from the first take to the final master.

Services

  • Mixing and mastering, attended or remote
  • Music production and arrangement
  • Studio and live recording
  • Sound design, editing and post-production
  • Original music for film, TV, commercials and games
